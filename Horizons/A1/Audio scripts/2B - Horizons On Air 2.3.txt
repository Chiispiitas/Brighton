HORIZONS A1 — LESSON 2B
TRACK 2.3 — HORIZONS ON AIR
EPISODE: BUS OR CAR?

Production note: one continuous show-style listening. Use one friendly host and three different street interview voices. Add a short HORIZONS ON AIR opening sting and light Manta street ambience between interviews. Keep the pace clear and natural for early A1, with short pauses after times and numbers. Approximate duration: 1:45–2:10.

[SFX: short show sting, then light street ambience]

HOST: Hello! This is Horizons On Air. Today: bus or car? We are in Manta, Ecuador.

HOST: Hi! What is your name?

JULIA: I’m Julia.

HOST: Julia, Moovit or inDrive?

JULIA: Moovit.

HOST: Moovit?

JULIA: Yes. It is an app. It is a bus app. I use Moovit.

HOST: What time is your bus?

JULIA: Six twenty.

HOST: What number?

JULIA: Fifteen.

HOST: Bus fifteen. Six twenty. Thank you.

JULIA: Thank you.

[short street ambience]

HOST: Hi! What is your name?

DIEGO: inDrive.

DIEGO: It is an app. It is a car app. I use inDrive.

HOST: What time is the car?

DIEGO: Six forty.

HOST: What number?

DIEGO: Six.

HOST: Car six. Six forty. Thank you.

DIEGO: Thank you.

[short street ambience]

HOST: Julia: bus fifteen at six twenty. Diego: car six at six forty. Ana: bus eight at seven thirty. Moovit is an app. inDrive is an app. Bus or car? Goodbye from Horizons On Air!
