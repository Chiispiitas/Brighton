Brighton A1 Listening Final Exam
Part 1 - People in the city square

[clear] Look at Part 1. Look at the picture. Listen and look. There is one example. [short pause]

[happy] This is a nice picture of the city square on Saturday.

[happy] Yes! A lot of my friends are here today.

[curious] Really? Can you see Mia?

[happy] Yes. Mia loves shopping. She’s trying on a hat at the market stall.

[curious] The woman by the hats?

[happy] That’s right. That’s Mia. [short pause]

[clear] This is the example. Now listen and choose the correct person for each name. [pause]

[clear] One. Leo.

[curious] Who’s the man near the bus stop?

[thoughtful] The man with the dark backpack?

[curious] Yes. He’s looking at his phone.

[happy] That’s Leo. He’s waiting for the bus.

[curious] Leo. Got it. [long pause]

[clear] Two. Ana.

[happy] Look at the woman at the market stall. She’s buying something.

[curious] Is she paying by credit card?

[happy] No. She’s paying with cash. That’s Ana.

[curious] Oh yes. Ana has a shopping bag too.

[happy] Exactly. [long pause]

[clear] Three. Diego.

[curious] Who’s the man on the bicycle?

[happy] The one next to the tree?

[curious] Yes. The blue bicycle.

[happy] That’s Diego. He goes bike riding every weekend.

[curious] Diego. Great. [long pause]

[clear] Four. Sofia.

[happy] Can you see Sofia?

[curious] Is she at the café?

[happy] No. Look near the museum. She has a camera.

[curious] Oh! The woman taking a photo?

[happy] Yes. That’s Sofia. [long pause]

[clear] Five. Carlos.

[curious] It’s starting to rain. Who has the umbrella?

[happy] That’s Carlos. He’s standing near the taxi.

[curious] The man with the umbrella next to the taxi?

[happy] Yes. That’s him. [long pause]
