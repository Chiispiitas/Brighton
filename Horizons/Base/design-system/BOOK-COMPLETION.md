# Horizons — complete Student’s Book standard

A finished Student’s Book needs a usable entrance, a coherent taught course and a useful reference ending. Read CANONICAL-STYLE.md, VISUAL-SPIRIT.md and the level’s current audit before applying this standard. The author’s 2 November 2026 completion request adds these requirements without authorizing a generic workbook or inserting teacher answers into the learner edition.

## Opening matter

Provide an original cover, a title/imprint, source-derived contents, a concise guide to the book and honest digital access. Preserve the series identity and verified authorship. The original Horizons cover informs the saturated magenta/orange identity and horizon metaphor; its watermarked stock photograph is reference material and must not be reused as a production asset. New cover art remains versioned, credited and documented.

Derive contents from actual current lesson titles, outcomes, topics and page destinations. Historical syllabuses and draft metadata are not pagination authorities. A contents table can be compact, but must remain readable and clickable in both the assembled viewer and PDF. Distinguish A/C language, B reading/listening and D speaking/writing without inventing features or preparatory requirements. Explain real book labels and audio track numbers with useful examples.

Print a real resource URL and a genuinely decoded QR code. Explain the controls that exist. Do not promise an app, access code, downloadable workbook, teacher platform or live Blooket games unless those resources actually exist. Future Blooket reserves remain blank. A short resource-entry URL must resolve to a maintained landing page or redirect rather than a missing directory.

## Reference ending

Use course-aligned useful-English phrases, pronunciation support and a manageable verb reference. Keep forms in real charts and examples in complete, aligned models. Past participles need not appear in an A1 reference when they are not taught. Avoid copying a higher-level appendix merely because it looks complete.

Include source-linked progress outcomes, a grammar finder, an alphabetical word finder, an audio finder and comfortable notes pages. Progress records ask learners to supply evidence or a useful example; they must not claim exam results or certification. Word lookup entries cover the study inventory, normalize legacy form-pair labels into sensible headwords and keep accurate current destinations. Audio lookup distinguishes visible track IDs from actual files, including legitimate reused-ID exceptions. Do not create a fake recording or silently change the established track mapping.

Keep original photographs, rights information and required attribution in end credits. Record generated art and author-supplied brand assets accurately. Do not invent ISBNs, collaborators, printing claims or permissions. Teacher keys and a separately authored workbook remain separate resources.

## Page and release contract

Register ordered opening, annex and closing masters in their manifests. Numbered pages form a consecutive sequence; covers and the title/imprint may use unique named identifiers with clear reader labels. Page IDs, printed footers, contents and internal destinations must agree. Preserve stable teaching-page numbers when new opening matter can occupy the reserved front folios.

New matter uses scoped styling and must not unintentionally change existing lessons or practice. Verify old content, media, response space and protected expressive objects. Reopen and inspect the final PDF, validate every destination and independently review new pages. Compare preserved teaching pages to the preceding edition. Verify the actual published entry page, viewer, audio, QR target and PDF bytes before declaring completion. Counts alone do not establish editorial quality.
