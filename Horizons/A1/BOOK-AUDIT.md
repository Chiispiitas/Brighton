## Grammar explanation standard

Compressed grammar prose is prohibited throughout the book, including main lessons, reviews, grammar practice and grammatical references elsewhere. Subject/verb arrow chains, contraction lists and inline “Usually: … Now: …” explanations must become real labelled charts or separately aligned complete models. Dots, slashes, arrows, bold text or arbitrary line breaks do not make an explanation a diagram. Use clear positive, negative and question columns when relevant. Show full and short forms in explicit columns. Keep genuine word movement, not insertion, spatial pictures and timelines where they explain the concept directly.

Prefer the smallest structure that makes the relationship clear. Do not add a redundant chart to every example, turn explanations into formula fragments or shrink teaching type and response fields to fit. Preserve actual reading/listening texts, task prompts, ordinary lexical definitions and conversational models. The shared [practice standard](../Base/design-system/PRACTICE-BANKS.md), [canonical style](../Base/design-system/CANONICAL-STYLE.md) and [component contract](../Base/design-system/component-contracts.md) enforce this distinction. The shared [grammar check](../Base/build/check-grammar-clarity.py) now runs in the build workflow; its pattern checks supplement human editorial review.
