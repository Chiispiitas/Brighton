BEYOND HORIZONS

Choose one. Try without help. Change roles and check together.

1New guest

Invent a name, a country and a job. Your partner asks three questions. Answer and spell the name.

Partner: Write the three answers. Read them back. Is the information correct?

2Look over there

Choose three things in the room. Describe them with this, that, these or those and a color.

Partner: Point to the things. Check each choice with your partner.

3Opening time

Write two times secretly. Say them to your partner. Then use one time for a new exhibition.

Partner: Write the times. Ask again if needed. Compare with the secret note.
