# Horizons — complete Student’s Book standard

A finished Student’s Book needs a usable entrance, a coherent taught course and an orderly ending. Read CANONICAL-STYLE.md, VISUAL-SPIRIT.md and the level’s current audit before applying this standard. The author’s 2 November 2026 correction supersedes the earlier completion pass: completeness does not mean adding every possible guide, index or appendix.

## Active edition scope

Retain front and back covers, a title page, an imprint, source-derived contents, two notes pages and updated end credits. Preserve the existing lessons, reviews, grammar practice, vocabulary practice and practical-language practice banks. Removing supplementary matter does not remove those authored practice banks.

How-to-use and digital-guide pages, Useful English, Sounds and past-verb appendices, Progress Records, and Grammar, Word and Audio Finders are excluded from the active edition unless the author explicitly requests them. Do not replace them with newly named sections or blank filler. Retain superseded source files and assets as reversible history where appropriate; omit them from active manifests, contents and navigation.

For the current A1 edition, the title and imprint occupy printed pages 1–2, contents 3–4, the preserved course and practice 5–151, notes 152–153 and credits 154–157. Only the two covers are unnumbered. This gives 157 numbered pages and 159 physical pages; it is the present source-derived arrangement, not a page quota for future books.

## Opening matter

Preserve the original cover’s features and brand typography. For A1 the protected wording is “Student’s Book” plus “Digital Pack”, “A1”, “BEGINNER”, “Series by David Santana” and the Wingfield name/logo. Do not substitute marketing claims or remove these features during a redesign. The HORIZONS wordmark uses the actual Bebas Neue Regular face, regular weight, with the cover’s slight −1.6° rise. Student’s Book and Digital Pack use the same face; the large A1/BEGINNER lockup uses Anton. Use the bundled native fonts and their recorded licences. Font substitutions, synthetic bold and a similar-looking generic condensed face are not acceptable brand cleanup. Verify the loaded fonts in rendered output.

Preserve the saturated magenta/orange identity and horizon metaphor. The original watermarked cover photograph is reference material and must not be reused as a production asset. The authorized replacement is a fresh image-generated mountain-view scene with the cyclist in side profile, the complete person in modest clothing, natural proportions and believable bicycle context. Keep the face, helmet and shoes clear. An intentional edge crop of the bicycle may serve the original cover composition; this does not relax complete picture evidence for teaching activities. Keep new cover art versioned, credited and documented. A change to the photograph does not authorize a change to the wordmark or feature wording.

Derive contents from actual current lesson titles, outcomes, topics and page destinations. Historical syllabuses and draft metadata are not pagination authorities. A contents table can be compact, but must remain readable and clickable in both the assembled viewer and PDF. Distinguish A/C language, B reading/listening and D speaking/writing without inventing features or preparatory requirements. Include only active sections in the directory.

Keep digital access truthful and compact within retained matter. A real imprint URL and the back-cover access route can serve the Digital Pack without inserting a separate guide. Any included QR code must decode to its actual maintained destination. Do not promise an app, access code, downloadable workbook, teacher platform or live Blooket games unless those resources actually exist. Future Blooket reserves remain blank. A short resource-entry URL must resolve to a maintained landing page or redirect rather than a missing directory.

## Notes and credits

Provide two comfortable notes pages with usable handwriting space. The current A1 pages have 28 rows each at 7.5mm; do not compress the lines or insert extra pages merely to meet a count. Retain accurate links to the existing grammar, vocabulary and practical-language practice banks. Preserve their actual printed-page and topic destinations, and the established recording mappings.

Keep original photographs, rights information and required attribution in end credits. Record active generated art, bundled font licences and author-supplied brand assets accurately; distinguish inactive historical artwork from the current edition. Do not invent ISBNs, collaborators, printing claims or permissions. Teacher keys and a separately authored workbook remain separate resources.

## Page and release contract

Register ordered opening, annex and closing masters in their manifests. Numbered pages form a consecutive sequence; the two covers use unique named identifiers with clear reader labels. Title and imprint are numbered in the current A1 edition. Page IDs, printed footers, contents and internal destinations must agree. Preserve stable teaching-page numbers when opening matter can occupy the reserved front folios.

New matter uses scoped styling and must not unintentionally change existing lessons or practice. Verify old content, media, response space and protected expressive objects. Reopen and inspect the final PDF, validate every destination and independently review new pages. Compare preserved teaching pages to the preceding edition. Verify the actual published entry page, viewer, audio, QR target and PDF bytes before declaring completion. Counts alone do not establish editorial quality.
