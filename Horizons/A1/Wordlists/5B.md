# 5B - Online shopping on air

Core pictured vocabulary: pay, try on, keep, return. Recycled clothes, colors, prices, work, notebook, phone, big/small, cheap/expensive and beautiful support comprehension. The explicit grammar remains too + adjective, adjective + enough, and not + adjective + enough. The earlier 4C enough-before-noun rule is not replaced.

5B is now listening-led. Lucy's show has two guests reviewing current online purchases and a listener-call segment. Gist and detail tasks precede brief language extracts, discovery and clarification. Natural current descriptions, genuine habitual use and present decisions replace any completed-purchase narrative in present simple. Keep/return decisions are explicit, and item/reason answers do not depend on untaught grammar.

Supported receptive extras include sleeve, pocket, beach bag, room for my feet, comfortable and I agree. The photographs, familiar item names and conversational context support these; they are not assessed new vocabulary or required student production. Occasional can/relative-clause chunks in guest speech are incidental, not new grammar targets. Every day recycles routines. No past-event sequencing, price arithmetic, retailer policy or actual purchase is required.

Printed language remains within the A1 ceiling. The show is not printed in full on the learner page. Two short exact quotes provide the evidence for too/enough; the complete teacher transcript and speech-only imports are available separately. The final message invites an independent opinion and reason, rather than a fill-in review.
