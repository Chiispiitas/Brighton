## Current design and teaching standard

- **Expressive content, coherent backbone.** Preserve crimson navigation, expressive alphabet/numeral objects, recurring identities and functional labels. Give source features their own appropriate shape, material and colour relationships. Avoid a repeated blue/green-plus-yellow treatment, universal rainbow, imposed mascot or neutralising cleanup.
- **Substantial and varied imagegen.** Use purposeful original source settings, concept objects, scenes, documents and cutouts. A speech pod can hold a grammatical model; a studio monitor can hold a portrait with source text below; a journal can hold a narrative. Keep approved people and useful photographic context. No rejected drawn-portrait substitutions.
- **Readable type and writing.** Ordinary tasks/models 10pt, instructions 9.2pt, word banks 9.5pt regular, Extra Practice 8.5pt. Source display/metadata and visual learning objects may use intentional sizes. White live content windows, contrast, whole faces, clear actions and exact quantities are essential. Normally 7mm handwriting rows and 6mm short fields. Recompose before reducing type or usable response space.
- **Clear teaching roles.** Grammar tools use cobalt; vocabulary green; communication/writing/skills plum; pronunciation warm orange. A custom concept object can replace the flat box surface while its functional header remains recognizable. Use words/shape alongside colour; never leak controlled answers through styling.
- **Useful language and tasks.** A/C develop language, B substantial reading/listening, D speaking/writing. Retain cumulative A1 load, genuine choice, information gaps, learner products and purposeful checks. This visual revision preserves all current learner words, answer keys, recordings and preferred original media.
- **Recurring book features.** Preserve the canonical main/secondary Horizons on Air identity, Jessica narration, established cast and receptive-skill mix. NEW WORDS belongs with actual input and selective bold colour; avoid bolding every word. Keep ordinary italic choices, familiar word banks, meaningful Extra Practice and named practice destinations. Credits stay at the end.
