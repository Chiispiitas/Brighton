FAMILY AT WORK

The Rivera family

This is Rivera Restaurant in Manta. It is a small family business. Diego and Elena work there from Monday to Saturday. The restaurant is busy in the afternoon.

Diego is a chef. He makes the food. Elena is a receptionist, and she talks to people at the restaurant. They like working together.

Diego is Elena's husband, and Elena is Diego's wife. Nico and Sofía are their children. They are students.

Nico and Sofía are at the restaurant on Saturdays. Nico is Elena's son, and Sofía is Diego's daughter.

Nico is Sofía's brother, and Sofía is Nico's sister. On Saturdays, they sit together at a table.

The family has some things at the restaurant. This is Diego's phone. These are Elena's keys. The blue notebook is Nico's notebook. Sofía's bottle is on the table.
