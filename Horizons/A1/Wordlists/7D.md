# 7D — An original diary entry

Explicit irregular pairs: get/got; go/went; have/had; see/saw; eat/ate; buy/bought; write/wrote. Daily chunks: got up, had breakfast, went to the bank/market/home, saw a friend, ate an apple, bought bread, wrote in a diary. These are modeled before independent writing; did/didn't still takes base verbs.

Sequence set: First, Then, After that, Finally. Support date/title and ordered events, without limiting students to a fill-in-the-gaps paragraph. Different/black/brown, small things, garden, cheese, fire, home and friend are recycled, image-supported or contextually explained; under the ground makes the Pepys hook concrete. The historical name is not assessed spelling. Avoid treating the public-domain portrait's music sheet as a diary. All vocabulary references and NEW WORDS badges sit at their meaningful input. No blanket highlighting/bolding. Communication - Diary writing accompanies the same-size concept models.
