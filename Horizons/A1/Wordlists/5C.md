# 5C - Shopping habits and places in town

Explicit new pictured vocabulary: supermarket, market, clothes store, library. These four places support the Unit 5 syllabus without pre-teaching the fuller town/mall vocabulary planned for Unit 6.

Explicit new frequency language: How often…?, once, twice, three times, every; a week, a month, a year. Week/day recycle routines; month/year are supported as the periods in the new frequency expressions. Once = one time; twice = two times; three or more uses times. Every + singular period, with no a. How often do you / does she recycle present-simple question patterns. Every Saturday and on Mondays recycle days of the week. Accept equivalent once a week / every week in comprehension without equating a month with exactly four weeks.

Recycled: go, buy, need, ask, read, shop, books, clothes, apples, tomatoes, fruit, vegetables, a lot of, never, do/does questions, third-person -s. Before I buy uses before already met as a time/position word; the repeated question Do I need them? makes the intended habitual meaning transparent. No present-tense narrative of completed purchases. Student source and audio describe genuinely recurring habits.

Minimalist living/spending habits are conveyed through using the library and asking whether clothes are needed. Minimalism/minimalist is teacher-facing context, not an assessed learner word. Shopping notes and habits are supported source labels. Ordinary commands use the established listen/repeat/match/read/circle/complete/write/ask/answer/tell frames. There are no hidden price arithmetic, month-name, loan-system or banking requirements.
