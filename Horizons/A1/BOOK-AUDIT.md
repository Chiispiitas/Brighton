## Teaching and design standards retained

Compressed inline grammar explanations remain prohibited book-wide. Subject/verb mappings, contraction chains and inline habit/now contrasts belong in labelled charts or aligned complete models. Dots, slashes, decorative arrows and bold text do not make a prose chain a diagram. Keep genuine word movement, insertion, spatial pictures and timelines where they explain the concept directly. Preserve real reading/listening texts, lexical definitions and learner tasks. Native reference tables use ordinary 10 pt models; expressive letters, phonetic targets, syllable stress and cover branding are deliberate display roles.
