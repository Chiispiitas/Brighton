Brighton A1 Listening Final Exam
Part 2 - Weekend trip to Manta

[clear] Look at Part 2. Listen and write. There is one example. [short pause]

[happy] I’m excited about our weekend in Manta!

[happy] Me too. Are we going by train?

[thoughtful] No. The train doesn’t go there. We’re going by bus.

[happy] By bus. Great. [short pause]

[clear] You can see the example. Now listen and complete Questions 6 to 10. [pause]

[clear] Six.

[curious] What’s the name of our hotel?

[happy] It’s the Blue Hotel.

[curious] Blue? Like the colour?

[happy] Yes. The Blue Hotel. It’s near the beach. [long pause]

[clear] Seven.

[curious] And what room are we in?

[thoughtful] Let me check... Room twenty-four.

[curious] Twenty-four, not forty-two?

[happy] Right. Room twenty-four. [long pause]

[clear] Eight.

[curious] What are we doing on Saturday morning?

[happy] We’re going to visit the museum.

[curious] The museum in the morning?

[happy] Yes. We can go shopping in the afternoon. [long pause]

[clear] Nine.

[curious] Where are we having lunch?

[happy] At a café near the park.

[curious] A restaurant?

[happy] No, a café. They have sandwiches, salad and orange juice. [long pause]

[clear] Ten.

[curious] And what are we doing on Sunday?

[happy] If the weather is sunny, we’re going swimming.

[curious] Swimming at the beach?

[happy] Yes. We can go bike riding another time. [long pause]
