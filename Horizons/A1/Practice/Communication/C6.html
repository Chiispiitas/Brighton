A visitor in your town

Make a visitor’s plan work 6D

Can I read books here? — Yes, you can. Go to the library.

Use this practice town. Today the museum is closed; the other five places are open.

Opposite: the café is opposite the library. Look across the street on the map.

1Start at the arrow, facing east. Complete the directions.

  1. Go straight and go past Oak Street. The park is on your .
  2. The café is the library.
  3. The museum is the pool.
  4. To walk north, turn at Oak Street.

2Help these visitors. Use the map and today’s opening information.

  1. “Can I go to the museum today?”
  2. “Where can I read a book?”
  3. “How do I get to the café from the start?”

3Choose privately. Give a partner a useful route.

Visitor

Secretly choose two things: read, swim, watch a movie, eat or walk. Ask where you can do them.

Helper

Suggest two open places. Give a route from the arrow to one. Ask the visitor to repeat the route.

On separate paper, draw the route. Compare the drawing with the spoken directions. Then change roles.
