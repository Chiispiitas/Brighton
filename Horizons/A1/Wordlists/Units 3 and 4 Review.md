# Units 3 & 4 Review — cumulative vocabulary

No new vocabulary set. Recycle Units 3–4:

- Daily routines: get up, go to work, go home, have breakfast/lunch/dinner; days of the week; always, usually, sometimes, never; before, after.
- Place descriptions: noisy, quiet, cheap and the other established adjectives.
- Food/drink: apples, bananas, eggs, rice, milk, water, bread, tomatoes, lemons, meat; countable/uncountable food senses.
- Flavors: sweet, salty, sour, spicy. The controlled odd-word item uses sour.
- Preparation and quantities: wash, cut, add, mix; too much, too many, enough, not enough; need.
- Writing: because, so; capital letters, full stops, question marks and the taught comma before so.

Minimal contextual support: cinema/film (the outdoor screen gives meaning) and sandwiches (transparent Spanish cognate). These words provide the new event setting; no item asks learners to recall an untaught spelling or infer a real person's background. Free retains its 4D $0/no-money meaning, now in a fictional cinema poster.

Instructions reuse the established read, look, match, write, complete, choose, circle, ask, use, plan and work in pairs. Category labels in activity 2 specify the intended classification so there is one controlled answer per row. There is no new NEW WORDS badge or back-of-book reference because this is cumulative review.
