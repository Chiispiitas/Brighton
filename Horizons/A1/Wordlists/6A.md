# 6A - Places in town 2

Six explicit new pictured places: **bank, pharmacy, post office, hospital, train station, museum**. The set extends supermarket, market, clothes store and library from 5C. Hospital/museum/pharmacy are transparent Spanish cognates; post office is anchored by the envelope scene and the new functional chunk. Train and station already appear in transport contexts, but train station is consolidated as a place name here.

Explicit functional vocabulary: **get cash; buy medicine; send a letter; take a train; look at art and history**. Cash/card recycle 5D. Medicine and art/history are cognate-supported and pictorially anchored. Send a letter is deliberately taught here, not assumed from the familiar alphabet sense of letter. Doctors/nurses and work recycle occupations and routines. No health advice or complaint vocabulary is introduced by the pharmacy context.

Supported context labels: city life, country life, small town, town center. New York/Stowe/Vermont/Grand Central/Recreation Path/Transit Museum/Vermont Ski and Snowboard Museum are real source names, not extra spelling targets. Recreation, transit and museum are transparent cognates; winter sports is supported by the named museum and is not a seasons syllabus. Country here means countryside, distinct from the countries sense in 1C. The photographed hills make the contrast visible. Read-aloud old paintings is receptive support for look at art, not a new assessed word.

Recycled structures: be, have/has, present-simple actions, want/need, do questions, because, let's + base verb (as a functional suggestion model), present continuous already available for photo discussion. Comparison is semantic big/small, not comparative -er. No existential there is/are rule, direction prepositions, ability can/cannot, future, past narrative or house/furniture syllabus. The source describes places and ordinary activities in the genuine present.

Instruction vocabulary reuses look/read/listen/repeat/match/write/choose/draw/label/work in pairs/tell/compare/why. New town center is the concrete purpose for the drawing; do not add planning/budget/municipality instructions. Students choose what to include together; no printed answer halves pretend to be private.
