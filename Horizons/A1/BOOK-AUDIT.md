Updated 2 November 2026. The compact practice edition saves five pages while preserving all teaching content. Source, layout and independently reviewed saved-PDF checks pass; published-viewer verification is pending.

## Practice composition standard

Two consecutive short lessons may share a physical page in the same bank and unit. Each section keeps its actual main lesson’s full header, code, title, objectives and typography: blue `#245b9a` for grammar, green `#247348` for vocabulary. Keep an obvious section boundary and reading order. Each lesson remains separately selectable in the viewer, including the second lesson on a shared page. A shared physical page is never a shared lesson identity.

Pair only when teaching type, complete pictures and approved response space fit comfortably. Ordinary concepts and study words remain at least 10 pt; instructions retain their defined role. Open writing rows stay at least 7 mm. Do not shrink content, remove substantive activities or interrupt a reference/task group to hit a page quota. Longer sets keep their own pages. Word banks remain on the same page as their tasks or immediately before them, within the same lesson. Memory tasks still require covering the word bank as well as their designated evidence.

Grammar retains concise positive/negative/question charts, complete models, selective changes and transformation arrows. Avoid inline rule paragraphs and redundant explanations. Vocabulary keeps the approved unique practice games, complete study vocabulary and useful pictures on every study/activity page. All pictorial assets use built-in imagegen; live charts, labels, arrows, grids and writing lines remain editable. Native generated files and alpha stay intact. No new artwork or audio was produced in this pass.
