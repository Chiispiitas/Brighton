## Permission and acquisition

Source and [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) checked October 10, 2026. The license permits free use and modification, including use in books; attribution is optional. Photographer credits are retained here and in the manifest. These are licensed photographs, not public-domain images. No subscription, API key or plugin was needed for this acquisition.

The photos illustrate original fictional teaching roles. The captions identify **the fictional story's** names and cities, not the model's identity, actual nationality, occupation or shooting location. No claim is made that the photographed people gave the podcast interviews or endorse Horizons. The neutral teaching context does not present people in an offensive or derogatory way. The images are integrated with original teaching text and activities rather than sold as standalone unaltered images.

`stock-photo-manifest.json` records source/download URLs, creators, dimensions, dates, use terms, file hashes and display crops. Photo 1 uses a portrait crop centered on the woman and books; photo 2 uses a landscape crop around the man at the desk; photo 3 retains the original 3:2 composition. Do not remove their backgrounds or use generative alteration.
