1Choose a useful reply. Circle the answer.

  1. Do you have any cheese? Yes, we do. / Yes, I’d like.
  2. Anything else? No, thanks. / No, I’m not.
  3. Can I pay by card? Yes, of course. / Here’s your bread.
  4. How much is it? It’s four dollars. / It’s a card.

3Open a new store. Prepare separately, then shop.

Customer

Secretly choose three foods. Ask about them. Choose another food if one isn’t available.

Shopkeeper

On separate paper, choose four foods and their prices. Choose cash, card or both. Keep your notes private.

Before you leave: Read back the foods, prices and payment method. Correct any mistake together. Change roles.
