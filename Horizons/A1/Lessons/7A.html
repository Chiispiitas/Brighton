Past

I / he / she / it was at home.

We weren’t online.

Questions and short answers

Were you at home?

Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.

Was the computer in the bedroom?

Yes, it was. / No, it wasn’t.

Where were you? At home.

We was at school. → We were at school.
