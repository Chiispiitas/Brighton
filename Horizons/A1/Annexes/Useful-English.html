Useful English

Ask, listen, check and keep talking.

Keep the conversation going. Try these phrases in class and outside class.

When you need help

ACan you repeat that, please?

BYes, of course.

ACan you speak more slowly, please?

BYes. No problem.

AI’m sorry. I don’t understand.

BLet’s look at an example.

Words and names

AHow do you spell Ana?

BA-N-A.

AHow do you say this in English?

BIt’s a notebook.

AWhat does “wallet” mean?

BIt’s a small thing for your money.

Working together

AAre you ready?

BYes. Let’s start.

AWhose turn is it?

BIt’s your turn.

AWhat do you think?

BI think this one is better.

Check the message

AIs that right?

BLet’s check.

ADo you mean Tuesday?

BYes, Tuesday.

ADid you say fifteen or fifty?

BFifteen. One-five.
