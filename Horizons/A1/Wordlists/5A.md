# 5A — Clothes, accessories and actions now

Core pictured vocabulary: T-shirt, shirt, sweater, jacket, jeans, dress, shoes, hat, scarf, bag. Bag recycles Unit 2. Jeans/shoes use plural agreement; a T-shirt / a shirt / a sweater / a jacket / a dress / a hat / a scarf / a bag are the audio models.

Grammar: present continuous for visible actions now; am/is/are + -ing; contracted affirmative/negative forms; inversion in yes/no and What ... doing? questions; short answers. Contrast I usually wear black with Now, I'm wearing blue. Only known regular -ing forms are required: wearing, looking, reading, buying, drinking, talking. No full spelling-change or future-arrangement syllabus.

Intentional contextual support: wear; buy; clothes; thrift shop; second-hand clothes; price/prices; now; just looking as a contextual shop chunk. Thrift shop is glossed next to the feature. Famous person, possessive 's, colors, numbers, usual routines, read/drink/talk, look at, before/after and basic personal questions recycle earlier lessons. The real-source label auction is transparent Spanish cognate; cents/dollar notation is interpreted from the paired prices and not assessed as a large-number production task.

Instruction language: listen, repeat, match, look, read, circle, complete, write, choose, work in pairs, ask/describe, draw, compare; notebook and photo are recycled classroom items. The model and physical comparison carry the private-photo task. The outcome does not require new fashion adjectives, past-tense narration, future plans, real shopping or a purchase.
