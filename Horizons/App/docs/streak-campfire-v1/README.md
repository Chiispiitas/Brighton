# Pico daily streak campfire production

This is a finite daily completion celebration, not an ambient replacement for the accepted Learn screen. Pico sits on a fixed wooden stump, roasts one marshmallow in a compact freestanding hearth, reacts when the flame catches, and settles. The streak number, weekdays and timing message are accessible HTML beside the illustration; they are never baked into a generated picture.

## Art and motion contract

- Keep the exact approved permanent faceless Pico head and separate neutral eyes, pupils, eyebrows/mouth, nose and eyelids. Their unchanged sources are pinned in `assets/art/streak-campfire-v1/baseline/`. One common affine registration retains every facial anchor. A new seated body does not authorize a new rabbit identity or a whole-head expression swap.
- Establish a complete seated pose as a geometry reference, then separately generate its stationary body/stump and two coherent clothed upper-body/paws/skewer/marshmallow keyposes. The roasting and raised/recoil keyposes are different authored images. The lower roasting skewer must physically reach the hearth opening; do not pull the shoulders apart to force the composition. A source-selected lower-body/stump underlay and front satchel preserve support and waist overlap.
- Keep each grip and held object together. No unsupported paw, floating skewer, detached shoulder, duplicate marshmallow or V-neck gap is acceptable.
- Generate only a compact individual hearth/log sprite and four individual native fire frames: ember, kindle, ignition and settled fire. Do not generate a room or a background scene. CSS supplies the paper ground.
- The seat, lower body, foreground satchel, hearth and logs stay fixed. The registered head/face moves independently; the coherent clothed upper-body keyposes receive only negligible local motion around the waist. Discrete keypose/expression/fire switches remain at full opacity. No fade, crossfade or glowing transparent motion layer is used.
- Reduced motion or a missing required part shows a prepared static final composition. The active illustration must not appear until every required part is decoded. Continue remains available during the finite animation.

## Source records

Every native call has its own `<part>.json` exact brief, `<part>-preflight.json` and `<part>-generation.json`. The preflight's actual `input_paths` are the paths attached to ImageGen, including edit targets; the three mandatory sheets R01/R02/R07 are retained on each call. Frozen exact sheet bytes and their original manifest are in `inputs/`.

The authored audit permits at most seven total image inputs, including targets. During this production the actual ImageGen tool rejected six paths before generation and reported an effective five-path limit. The revised down-right roast call therefore retains all three mandatory sheets and the exact body/paw targets, omitting the redundant complete canonical. The validation failure is recorded in `arm-roast-down-input-cap-failed.json`. No image was created by that attempt.

There are 14 unchanged native outputs, 17 generation attempts including three failures without an image, and one additional tool input-validation failure before generation. The first transparent forelimb groups and excited glyph plate are the three rejected native images, for broad glow/scattered semitransparent exterior pixels. They stay untouched in `native/` for provenance. Clean replacements are deliberately generated against opaque solid magenta. The clean raised forelimb-only output is retained as a superseded source; the final active keyposes are the coherent clothed upper-body sprites. `tools/prepare-streak-campfire.py` removes only the declared key-color background, crops/selects source pixels, and uniformly scales/translates existing native pixels. It does not draw a rabbit, new anatomy, flames or replacement facial strokes.

Three benign isolated cartoon-rabbit forelimb requests failed at ImageGen output moderation with HTTP400/category `sexual`: requests `7b850a47-2964-4d62-8cf0-fd68b4c6a419`, `922a28ff-2ee5-46f0-a5ae-c2ceb7c67bf7` and `149503b6-f845-4006-a9e4-3f5239523117`. Their `*-failed-generation.json` records preserve each exact prompt, actual input paths, failing operation and absence of image output. Isolated-forelimb retries stopped. The successful final hybrid keyposes are natural clothed torso sprites with attached cartoon paws and held skewer. These were image-generation failures, not application, save or backend failures.

## Reproduction and quality gate

1. Pin and inspect the accepted character, permanent blank head and individual neutral face layers before changing a pose. Copy original bytes; do not overwrite accepted sources.
2. Plan what is stationary and what moves. Define shoulder/neck/grip anchors and authored keyposes. Test whether the prop actually reaches the interaction target before animating.
3. Write one concrete brief per native sprite or separate pose, run `tools/check-image-brief.py`, and attach its returned paths. Include mandatory reference sheets and exact target images. Honor both the authored seven-input ceiling and any smaller effective tool limit.
4. Review each raw output. Reject changed character identity, contour drift, missing forelimbs, detached grips, holes, broad halos, transparent interiors and unwanted scene chunks. Keep failures with their actual reason and regenerate the part. Use an explicitly declared opaque intermediate when native transparency is defective.
5. Save unchanged native outputs, exact prompts, actual references, preflights and failure evidence. Register parts uniformly on the shared 760×500 transparent canvas. Keep the original blank head and all inherited face layers on the same transform; separately register new expression glyphs at those anchors.
6. Reconstruct neutral roast and ignited/lifted states before motion. Compare the head to the accepted still, inspect shoulder/neck seams, clear single-object grips, hearth alignment, full opacity and static supports. A numeric bounds check does not replace direct visual review.
7. Animate the shared finite clock with discrete authored pose/fire/expression swaps and local part transforms. Inspect true extremes and normal speed at actual desktop/mobile sizes. Verify reduced motion, decode failure, disposal, visibility pause and immediate Continue, then test the offline cache with exact final bytes.
8. Update the main animation audit and Image Production guide with current references, rejected outputs, registration details and known limits. Refresh the mandatory reference pack only after tracked renderer changes are finished.
