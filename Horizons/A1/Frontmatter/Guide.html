Your book

How to use Horizons

Eight units. Four lessons in each unit. English you can use.

Find your way around a unit

AC

Language

See useful words and grammar in context. Look at the examples, then try the language yourself.

B

Reading or listening

Read a text or listen to a conversation. Find the main idea, then look or listen again for details.

D

Speaking or writing

Use your English for a real purpose: make a plan, have a conversation or write something useful.

Look for these features

NEW WORDS

Notice new words in the text. Study the word bank, then practice the words.

♪ 1.1 Audio

The number tells you which track to play. Listen again to check your answers or practice.

GRAMMAR

Study the chart or picture. Notice the words in bold. Use the complete examples to help you.

Go to: Grammar / Vocabulary

Follow the page link for more help and practice. Each section has the same lesson code.

EXTRA PRACTICE

Use English again in a new way. Write a message, ask someone a question or make something useful.

BEYOND HORIZONS

Try a new challenge in each Review and practice. Choose your own ideas and use what you know.

After two units

A Review and practice brings the language together in new situations. Use it to see what you know and what to practice next.
