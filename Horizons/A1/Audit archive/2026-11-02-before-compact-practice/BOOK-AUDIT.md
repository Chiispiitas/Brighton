Editable charts, transformation arrows, labels, game grids and writing lines remain live. Retain native transparency. Avoid a second generic cue when the combined lesson already has a meaningful picture. Grammar keeps compact positive/negative/question charts, clear transformations and complete models; no inline explanatory rule paragraphs or decorative photos on form-only pages. Ordinary study words remain10pt or larger. Preserve generous writing space.
