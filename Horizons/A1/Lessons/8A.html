Sam’s travel vlog · after the trip

A city with animals

Last week

Last week, I traveled to Toronto by train. I stayed for two nights in a small hotel.

09:00

On my first day, I arrived at nine and left my bag at the hotel.

10:00

At ten, I visited the waterfront and took photos of the tall buildings.

Afternoon

In the afternoon, I took a boat to the islands. I saw ducks and squirrels there.

Evening

In the evening, I ate at a market. A big city can have animals, too!

Original classroom vlog. Travel-film inspiration: Expedia’s Toronto guide; city facts: City of Toronto.
