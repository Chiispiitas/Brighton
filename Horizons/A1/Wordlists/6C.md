# 6C - Movement and directions

Explicit: **go along the street; go across the street; go through the park; go past the bank; go straight; turn left; turn right**. **On foot = walking** is glossed at first input. Across crosses sides, along follows the length, through passes inside, past goes beyond. All are supported by actual direction arrows, a picture family and/or the practice map. First input carries NEW WORDS; Vocabulary - Movement and directions and Grammar - Giving directions references are nearby.

Recycled: town places, park, supermarket, bank, library, café, museum, near/far, next to, on your left/right, walk, school, minutes, numbers, what/where, giving real personal reasons. Straight/left/right are clarified by functional arrows, not by a new metalanguage vocabulary set. The base-form command is new explicit language; no modal can or past forms are introduced. No behind/between/opposite or additional direction taxonomy is required.

Source support: Walk Score is a proper product name; the A1 paraphrase explains 0-100 points for an address and nearby shops/services. Address, number, points/services use prior/simple/cognate context. A high-level technical analysis of pedestrian density or an actual location score is not required. Final learner 1-5 points rate a particular walk subjectively and are not an official Walk Score. Directions in the model are fictional, not public navigation.
