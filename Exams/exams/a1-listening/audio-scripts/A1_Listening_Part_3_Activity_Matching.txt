Part 3 - Family weekend activities

[clear] Look at Part 3. Nina is telling Marco about her family and their favourite weekend activities. Listen and choose the correct activity for each person. There is one example. [short pause]

[curious] What do your parents usually do on the weekend, Nina?

[happy] They love having a barbecue at home. My father cooks and my mother makes salad.

[happy] That sounds nice!

[clear] You can see the example. Her parents have a barbecue. Now listen to Questions 11 to 15. [pause]

[clear] Eleven. Her uncle.

[curious] What about your uncle?

[happy] Uncle Luis loves the mountains. He goes hiking almost every Sunday.

[curious] Does he go swimming too?

[thoughtful] Not often. Hiking is his favourite. [long pause]

[clear] Twelve. Her brother.

[curious] And your brother?

[excited] Mateo loves soccer. He plays with his friends in the park every Saturday.

[curious] Does he watch movies with you?

[happy] Sometimes, but he always chooses soccer first. [long pause]

[clear] Thirteen. Her sister.

[curious] What does your sister like doing?

[happy] Carla likes quiet activities. She plays chess with our grandfather.

[curious] Chess? Is she good?

[proud] Very good! She wins a lot. [long pause]

[clear] Fourteen. Her cousin.

[curious] And your cousin Daniela?

[happy] She has a new bike. She goes bike riding near the beach.

[curious] Every weekend?

[happy] Almost every weekend when the weather is good. [long pause]

[clear] Fifteen. Her grandmother.

[curious] What does your grandmother do for fun?

[thoughtful] She loves art and history. She visits the museum a lot.

[curious] Does she go hiking with your uncle?

[happy] No, the museum is her favourite place. [long pause]
