# 6D - Helping a visitor

Explicit photo-supported nouns: **waterfall, garden, roller coaster, mall**. **Mall = shopping center**; both places shown have stores. Meaningful chunks: **see a waterfall; walk in a garden; ride a roller coaster; go shopping**. **Closed = not open** is a local gloss at the visitor listening, not a hidden museum-hours inference. NEW WORDS accompanies first input and the nearby Vocabulary - Shopping centers and attractions reference.

Grammar: **can / can't + base verb** for an available action or personal availability; **Can + subject + verb?**; **Yes, I/you can. No, I/you can't.** No -s on can or following verb, no do/does before can, no to before the base verb. Third-person singular present and days explain why Grace can't come on Saturday. Can't does not automatically mean a legal prohibition: this lesson uses availability, not invented laws/safety rules. Nearby Grammar - Can and can't and Communication - Helping a visitor references accompany input/transfer.

Recycled: places, city, food, clothing/shopping context, go/walk/see/eat/watch, days, free time, directions from 6C, near/next to/in, question frames, private choices and a shared plan. Singapore/USA and the venue names are identifiers, not new country spelling tests. Indoor is understood through the photographs and 'inside' in teacher support; it is not an extra assessed set. Visit/local/art are cognate or prior context. The final independent speaking output uses present/modal language; no future or past syllabus is smuggled in. Private note/table space supports, rather than scripts, the conversation.
