# 7A — When I was little…

Language A: past of be, childhood and past-time expressions. Printed pages 59–60.

1. A: living room, 1985. B: bedroom, 2005. These are commissioned fictional reconstructions, not documentary photos or evidence of universal lifestyles in either decade. Describe the actual scenes rather than claim that all families had the same technology.
2. Track 7.1: yesterday, last night, last weekend, last year. The worked examples explicitly set Monday and 2026; they do not describe the day this lesson is taught. Last weekend = Saturday and Sunday in this example, not a claim about every country's workweek. Ask students to adapt the expressions to their own local calendar.

4. 1 was; 2 we were not; 3 before.
5. 1 was; 2 were; 3 Was; 4 wasn't; 5 Were; 6 weren't. The instruction requires was/were except in the explicit negative answers after No; it does not invite a choice between equally grammatical invented positive/negative facts.
6. Track 7.3: 1 at the library; 2 a woman; 3 at the park. The first reply explicitly rejects home, the second confirms a woman, the third explicitly rejects school. Adeline/Mark Natural are fictional practice roles, not the established named interviewees.
7. Personal answers. Students choose private childhood information, ask real questions, and write two original sentences about the partner. Respect a preference to discuss an invented person or remembered place rather than disclose private childhood details. The shared photo is a prompt, not the partners' answers.
