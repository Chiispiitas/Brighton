# 8C — Body parts, health complaints and changing a plan

Body parts: foot, hand, head, leg, stomach, arm. Six live pointer labels and a fully clothed photographic reference. Complaints: a cold, a headache, a stomachache, a toothache. Model chunks: I have a ... / She has a ...; My head/arm/stomach hurts; My legs hurt. Retain a with these countable complaint nouns, and keep singular/plural subject agreement explicit.

Functional rescheduling: Can you go tomorrow? I'm sorry, I can't. Can we go on Sunday? What time? Let's leave at ten. Can and basic present/past are recycled; body/health vocabulary is the language focus. Real Raducanu foot-related withdrawal supplies a short fact-based hook; all trip messages and acted models are fictional and no diagnoses/treatments are taught.

Three recordings 8.7–8.9 match the words/dialogue/models; no unspoken extension is claimed as recorded. Normal natural input, selective target emphasis only in the concept box. Learners can invent a problem for the private rescheduling task.
