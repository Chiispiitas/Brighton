TRACK 5.5

LUCY / NARRATOR: Track five point five.
LUCY / NARRATOR: Welcome back to Horizons On Air! I’m Lucy. Today: Keep or return? You see something online. The color is beautiful, and the price is good. But is it right for you? Mina and Tess are here with two shopping reviews. Hi, both of you!
MINA: Hi, Lucy!
TESS: Hi! And I have a very big jacket problem.
LUCY / NARRATOR: Oh no! Well, let’s start with something good. Mina, tell us about your bag.
MINA: This is my favorite bag! I love the blue color. The bag is big enough for my notebook. I use it for work.
LUCY / NARRATOR: It looks good. And the price?
MINA: Only eighteen dollars. It isn’t too expensive.
TESS: Eighteen dollars? That’s good! Is it a beach bag?
MINA: Not for me! It’s my work bag. My notebook goes in here, and my phone goes in this little pocket.
LUCY / NARRATOR: So, keep or return?
MINA: Keep! I use it every day.
LUCY / NARRATOR: Now, Tess. You’re wearing an orange jacket. I like the color.
TESS: Me too! The color is beautiful. But look at the sleeves. Where are my hands?
LUCY / NARRATOR: Oh, yes. Those sleeves are very long!
TESS: The jacket is too big for me. I don’t wear big jackets. It isn’t good for me.
LUCY / NARRATOR: Is the color a good reason to keep it?
TESS: No! I like the color, but I don’t like the size.
MINA: I agree. You need a jacket you can wear!
LUCY / NARRATOR: Keep or return, Tess?
TESS: Return! A beautiful color isn’t enough.
LUCY / NARRATOR: Exactly! A good price and a beautiful color are nice. But the item needs to be right for you. Mina, Tess, thank you!
MINA: Thanks, Lucy!
TESS: Thank you!
LUCY / NARRATOR: And what about you? Do you keep it, or return it? Our listeners are next. This is Horizons On Air!
