HANNE’S TINY HOME · BELGIUM · 2023

Room for life

27square meters

Hanne’s home in Belgium is only 27 square meters. It has a kitchen, a bathroom, a living room and a bed. The kitchen and living room are in one open area. The bed is above a space for her things.

Hanne writes for work. She has the things she needs. She does not want a big house and a full-time job. She wants more time for walks in nature and for travel.

For Hanne, one thing is difficult: big groups of friends do not fit inside. For barbecues, her friends meet outside. For Hanne, a tiny home is about choosing what she needs and having time for life.

free time = time for things you like   ·   tiny = very small

Adapted from Hanne’s interview, Tiny House Talk, 8 December 2023.
