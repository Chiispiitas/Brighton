GRAMMAR: PRESENT CONTINUOUS

For actions now: use am, is or are and a verb ending in -ing.

AffirmativeNegative
II’m wearing a hat.I’m not wearing a hat.
he / she / itShe’s reading.She isn’t reading.
you / we / theyThey’re talking.They aren’t talking.

Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.

Is he reading?
