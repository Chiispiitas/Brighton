HORIZONS A1 — LESSON 2A
LOOK INSIDE MY BAG!

Production note: Use two friendly young-adult voices. Keep the pace clear and natural for early A1. Pause briefly between the six items in Track 2.2.

==================================================
TRACK 2.1 — READ AND LISTEN
==================================================

NORA: Look inside my bag!
ELI: What is this?
NORA: This is my phone.
ELI: What are these?
NORA: These are my keys.
ELI: What is that?
NORA: That is my notebook.
ELI: What are those?
NORA: Those are my headphones.
ELI: Nice!
