# Horizons authoring instructions — every level

Before authoring or redesigning a book, read `Base/design-system/VISUAL-SPIRIT.md`, `CANONICAL-STYLE.md`, `GUIDED-DISCOVERY.md`, `component-contracts.md`, `asset-policy.md` and that book's current audit. Relative design-system filenames in this sentence are inside `Base/design-system/`.

The author's own B2 book is the primary emotional reference. Preserve warm adult playfulness, saturated local colour, expressive letter/number objects, physical concept metaphors and purposeful cutouts. Functional type, navigation and recurring identities stay coherent; individual source features keep suitable visual worlds. Do not neutralise approved details as generic cleanup, impose a universal rainbow or repeat one blue/green-plus-yellow treatment.

Consult the book's `Sources/Visual-personality.json` before modifying a spread. Record its visual idea and protected elements, with intentional display-type exceptions. Create the equivalent register for every new level. Meaningful teaching, adult suitability, readable contrasts, complete image evidence and generous response space govern the judgment. Preserve the approved source words, keys, recordings and preferred imagery unless the task authorizes their revision.

Run the shared visual-spirit coverage and configured object checks, relevant layout/source checks and an independent saved-PDF inspection. Inspect neighbouring spreads for visual fatigue and verify the published viewer when publishing is in scope. A passing counter or geometry check is not an artistic judgment. Record actual evidence and keep a reversible baseline.

The author has delegated editorial authority. These instructions do not add a new approval step for routine, reversible work within the authorized task.

When authoring or revising practice banks, also read `Base/design-system/PRACTICE-BANKS.md`. Inspect all empty response fields in the saved PDF; visible-text overflow checks alone cannot prove that writing lines fit.
