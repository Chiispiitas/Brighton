# Horizons print QA checklist

Run this checklist before a lesson/spread is approved for publication.

## Content protection

- [ ] Syllabus title, grammar, vocabulary field and real-world context are unchanged.
- [ ] No frozen pre-existing Student's Book page has been rewritten or replaced without explicit authorization.
- [ ] American English is consistent.
- [ ] Productive language remains appropriate for A1.

## Exercise flow

- [ ] All numbered exercises on each page are in one vertical sequence.
- [ ] Exercise numbers are in numerical order.
- [ ] Any 2–3 column layout occurs only inside one exercise body.
- [ ] Subparts A/B/C are visually subordinate to the exercise number.

## Spread design

- [ ] The two pages form a deliberate spread rather than two unrelated pages.
- [ ] Page 1 and Page 2 have useful contrast in density or visual emphasis.
- [ ] Important text/faces are kept away from the conceptual gutter.
- [ ] Full-bleed or frame-breaking elements are intentional and do not interfere with interaction mechanics.

## Typography

- [ ] Lesson title hierarchy is clear without relying on decoration.
- [ ] Reading measure is comfortable.
- [ ] Long text has no obvious widows/orphans where they can be avoided.
- [ ] Body text is not reduced merely to make content fit.
- [ ] Captions, instructions and references remain legible at actual A4 print size.
- [ ] Emphasis styles are consistent.

## Photography

- [ ] Every image has a pedagogical/editorial reason to exist.
- [ ] Crop direction supports the page composition.
- [ ] Faces and key objects are not covered by overlays.
- [ ] Text-over-photo has sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Image source/license is recorded.
- [ ] Production image is local, not hotlinked.
- [ ] Effective print resolution is acceptable (target 300 ppi where practical).
- [ ] No watermarks or preview assets remain.

## Grammar / pronunciation

- [ ] Grammar and Pronunciation are visually distinguishable.
- [ ] Grammar tables/examples are accurate to the locked syllabus.
- [ ] Pronunciation stress/sound notation is clear and consistent.

## Audio / QR

- [ ] Printed track number matches the internal audio ID.
- [ ] QR placement is not too close to trim or folds.
- [ ] QR is large enough to scan in print.
- [ ] Audio script and voice metadata are recorded.

## Layout and print

- [ ] Page is exactly A4 at 100% scale.
- [ ] No content overflows the fixed page frame.
- [ ] No unintended browser header/footer appears.
- [ ] Background graphics are preserved in PDF output.
- [ ] All intended page breaks occur correctly.
- [ ] Exercise/focus blocks do not split unexpectedly.
- [ ] Debug/QA overlays are disabled in final output.

## Accessibility / digital future

- [ ] Images have meaningful alt text in production HTML.
- [ ] Decorative imagery has empty alt text when appropriate.
- [ ] Track numbers and instructions are real text, not baked into images.
- [ ] Printed answer mechanics can later map to actual form controls in digital exercises.
- [ ] Semantic IDs are stable and unique.

## Final spread review

Print or view the two pages side-by-side at realistic physical size. Check visual balance from a distance, then read every instruction and activity at normal reading distance. Approval requires both editorial balance and classroom usability.
