Track eight point two.

Last week, I traveled to Toronto by train. I stayed for two nights in a small hotel. On my first day, I arrived at nine and left my bag at the hotel. At ten, I visited the waterfront and took photos of the tall buildings. In the afternoon, I took a boat to the islands. I saw ducks and squirrels there. In the evening, I ate at a market. A big city can have animals, too!
