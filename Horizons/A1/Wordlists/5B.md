# 5B - Shopping reviews

Core pictured vocabulary: pay, try on, keep, return. Buy, choose, clothes, jacket, bag, shoes, sweater, dress, colors, work, notebook, price and favorite recycle earlier material. Online shopping is the unit context; review/reviews has already appeared in 3C. Customer is an unassessed source heading supported by the pictured shopping actions and recognizable review-site frame. Pay and return are made explicit by the card/payment and parcel-counter photos; keep contrasts with return.

Grammar support to reading/listening: too + adjective; adjective + enough; not + adjective + enough. Compare too big with big enough and isn't big enough. The enough-before-noun pattern from 4C is not replaced: explain the contrast when needed, but this focus is adjective position. Too meaning also is not assessed. Big/small, cheap/expensive and beautiful are recycled; no new size system or fashion-adjective syllabus is imposed.

Reading uses present-simple purchase accounts rather than unsupported past-tense narration. At home / for work / for me / colors / eighteen dollars are previously accessible language. The controlled listening recycles the present continuous from 5A. Instructions use the established listen/read/match/circle/complete/write/choose/work in pairs frames. The final review allows independent expression; no sentence-gap template is used.
