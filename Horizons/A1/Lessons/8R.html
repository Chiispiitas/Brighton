BEYOND HORIZONS

Choose one. Try without help. Change roles and check together.

1Three moments

Say where you were yesterday and two things you did. Real or invented? Your partner decides.

Partner: Ask two questions with Was…?, Were…? or Did…? Then check the story.

2Change the plan

Privately choose two busy days. Find a free day for an art class together. Then change the plan and give a reason.

Partner: Agree on the new day. Repeat the plan to check it.

3Take a message

Choose a private name, time and price. Make a short call about a class. Your partner takes a message.

Partner: Write the three details. Ask to repeat or spell. Read the message back.
