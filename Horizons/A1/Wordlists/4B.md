# 4B — Flavors and food opinions

Core vocabulary: salty, sweet, sour, spicy; tasty (lesson title).

Essential pictured/context vocabulary: vegan, plant/plants, meat, burger, chips, ice cream, strawberry/strawberries, chili pepper/chili peppers, lemon, fries, menu.

Supporting verbs used in the reading/models: help, give, make, ask. Keep instruction verbs from the established bank: listen, repeat, match, read, look, circle, complete, write, work in pairs, find.

Grammar: like / don't like (Unit 3 present-simple recycling); object pronouns me, you, him, her, it, us, them. Recycle food countability, adjectives and personal questions. No like + -ing or 4C/4D grammar expansion.
