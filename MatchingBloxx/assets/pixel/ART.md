# Matching Bloxx sprite set

All source sheets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this game. Native sprite atlases are `town.png` and `compact.png`. The other PNGs in this folder are composed from the same art.

The art was aligned to a shared pixel grid, reduced to a common limited palette, and checked against a contrasting matte at 3× nearest-neighbor scale. Both sheets use only fully transparent and fully opaque pixels.

## City source prompt

> Create a single transparent PNG sprite sheet for original retro handheld city stacking arcade game MATCHING BLOXX. This is a production sprite atlas, no text. Strictly FOUR separate buildings at the same ground baseline in a single wide row with large completely transparent gutters. Left to right: 1 compact two storey terracotta brick corner bakery with ochre awning, no sign lettering; 2 medium four storey warm buff plaster apartment with teal shutters and tiny rooftop chimney; 3 taller slender seven storey sage grey hotel apartment with rooftop water tank; 4 low teal-roof civic building with square windows and a slender corner spire. Architectures should read as one small coastal city, European mixed masonry, muted terracotta rose, warm sandstone gold and dusty slate teal. Architectural VIEW straight-on facade orthographic, tiny roof visible from above, right side at most one sixth width, correctly matched scale in all four sprites. Native sprite widths analogous to 26, 30, 28, 34 pixels. True deliberate structured pixel art look, hard squared and stepped pixel edges, absolutely no blur, dithering, photoreal, noise, or individual glowing lines. Use broad flat 3-shade surfaces, window grids aligned cleanly, thick visual masses and light from upper left. BIG clean truly transparent margins around the sprites, no landscape, no ground, no visible matte, no shadows outside the silhouettes. FOUR islands only; no text, labels, trim, decorative framing or dotted markers.

## Tower source prompt

> Create one transparent game sprite sheet for a retro handheld pixel construction arcade game. FOUR isolated game entities on a single wide horizontal row, same baseline, very wide completely transparent gutters, no letters/numbers. Left to right (1) one square stackable single apartment floor segment, warm ochre cream lintel and pale terracotta exterior, dark slate teal panes with exactly three square front windows, front facing with small right wall, flat top narrow roof lip; (2) exactly the same box width, height, view and wall palette but as a ground-floor entry with a centered double door and a small dark step, one window on each side; (3) a low stepped slate teal penthouse roof unit with warm gold trim, roof cap and a tiny antenna, similar footprint, roughly half the visual height of the wall segments excluding antenna; (4) a thin flat concrete foundation platform at the same width with bevelled slate edge and narrow amber stripe; low height. The 2D sprites are nearly straight-on facades with right side at most a sixth of total width; show slight depth on top and keep verticals straight. The first and second bodies are identical rectangular envelopes roughly 5:4 width to height including depth; cut off any imaginary floors above and below. Flat carefully constrained earthy palette of terracotta #c27559, sandstone #e6b77a, dark slate teal #344f54, and warm cream, consistent upper left lighting. True native 16-bit pixel art, strong SIMPLE silhouette, clean intentional stepped square pixels, crisp 1-pixel edges, almost no pattern or texture. No greyscale, no gradients, no noise, no softened pixels, no shadows outside. Transparent alpha background with exactly four sprite islands.

## Site source prompt

> Generate one transparent game sprite sheet, wide horizontal row, exactly SIX isolated elements with big transparent gutters, no ground, no text, no frame. Pixel art for the same 16-bit retro handheld coastal city construction arcade game, muted ochre sandstone and terracotta, slate teal outlines, upper left light, hard pixels, restrained handful of flat colors per object, no gradients, no external shadows, no blur/noise. Left to right: (1) a long HORIZONTAL side-on tower crane boom consisting of repeating amber lattice X truss modules with see-through transparent holes between braces and two solid parallel rails, 5 times wider than it is tall, must be perfectly level; (2) a compact horizontal gold trolley housing for it with two tiny dark wheels and dark underslung connector, separate tiny single cast-metal hook below as part of its silhouette; (3) a leafy round muted green street tree with short trunk; (4) an amber cream construction sawhorse with 2 bands, one clean silhouette; (5) stacked terracotta bricks on a small wooden pallet; (6) two small ochre traffic cones on charcoal feet touching the same base line. Authentic intentional game-ready low resolution square pixel construction with side-on facade view and slight right wall, appropriate for the bottom of a street. Keep six individual islands from touching anything; transparent background; no guides, labels, borders or extra objects.
