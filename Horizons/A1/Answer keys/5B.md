# Horizons A1 - 5B: That's my favorite!

Student's Book pages 43-44. Reading/listening lesson with supporting shopping vocabulary and adjective patterns.

1. 1 return D; 2 pay A; 3 try on B; 4 keep C. Return and keep contrast; the parcel-counter scene is returning a purchase, not buying it.
2. Tess returns the jacket. Gist listening/reading before detailed retrieval.
3. 1 Mina; 2 Tess; 3 Mina; 4 Tess.
4. 1 a problem; 2 after.
5. 1 too big; 2 big enough. A has fallen shoulders and cuffs covering the hands. B has normal shoulders and cuffs at the wrists. The two scenes show the same fictional Tess; the purple jacket is a separate try-on comparison, not a claim about her original orange-jacket review.
6. 1 shoes: keep / big enough; 2 dress: return / too small; 3 sweater: keep / cheap enough. Accept It isn't big enough as an accurate expanded explanation for the dress, though the supplied bank uses too small. The listening gives new evidence, not answers copied from the printed reviews.
7. Open response. A short review should identify an item and give a clear opinion with a reason. Encourage too or enough when meaningful; do not force a negative review or penalize an honest positive one. Partner reading uses personal information the other learner does not already hold.

Extra Practice: an independent online review and two adjectives; no purchase, registration or paid account required.
