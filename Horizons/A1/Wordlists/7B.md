# 7B — Months, dates and ago

Months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

Explicit ordinal models: first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth, eleventh, twelfth; twentieth, twenty-first, twenty-second, twenty-third; thirtieth, thirty-first. Most regular dates add -th; 1st/2nd/3rd exceptions and compound forms are visibly modeled, including 11th/12th. Say July twelfth or the twelfth of July. The written 12th is not cardinal twelve when saying a date.

Ago = before now, follows duration: one day ago / six days ago. Date anchors are episode July 12, 2026; July 11 one day ago; July 10 two days ago. In + month/year; on + date; When were you born? All target input has NEW WORDS and an appropriate Vocabulary/Grammar reference. No blanket bolding or colored highlighting; word-bank conventions apply only to actual word banks. Birthday, artist, actor, family, restaurant, small/good and father/teacher recycle familiar language. The real celebrity names are identifiers, not hidden spelling tests. No arithmetic age calculations, advanced biography comprehension, celebrity impersonation or reported-speech syllabus.
