BEYOND HORIZONS

Choose one. Try without help. Change roles and check together.

1Find the guest

Secretly choose someone in activity 4. Describe the clothes and the action. Do not say the name.

Partner: Name the person. Check the clothes and action against the picture.

2Keep or return?

Choose a real or imaginary item of clothing. Keep it or return it? Explain with too or enough.

Partner: Ask about the price and how often your partner wears it.

3Local guide

Choose a place in activity 5, except the station. Give directions from the station. Do not name it.

Partner: Follow the route. Name the place and say what you can do there.
