## Real story and fictional boundaries

The compact One sweater. Two prices. panel gives a real reported thrift-shop find associated with Vince Lombardi: Goodwill, 2014, 58 cents; Heritage Auctions, 2015, $43,020. The source is Heritage Auctions' 24 February 2015 release, describing the 21 February sale and the preceding summer's discovery. Its lead reports the item at 58 cents; later it describes Goodwill's 58-cents-per-pound pricing. Treat 58 cents as the source's reported acquisition price, not a general price for any sweater or a current shop offer. The source also identifies Sean and Rikki McEvoy as the finders and the sweater as black/gold; the synthetic red sweater in the vocabulary grid is **not** a reconstruction of that historical garment.
