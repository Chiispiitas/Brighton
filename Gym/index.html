Progression rules

How the plan advances

1. Complete clean repsOnly progress when form stays controlled and pain-free.
2. Add reps before difficultyAdd 1–2 reps first, then choose a harder variation.
3. Skill before fatiguePractice kip-ups and handstands while fresh, not at the end of exhaustion.
4. Deload every 4th weekThe app reduces volume to help recovery and prevent sudden spikes.
