TRACK 1.8 — EXERCISE 4.1: READ AND LISTEN
==================================================

TOM: Hey! I’m Tom.
TOM: I’m a taxi driver. I drive a taxi in the morning. But in the evening, I’m a teacher!
TOM: I teach English to my students. They’re smart!

SARAH: Hi! I’m Sarah.
SARAH: I’m a doctor, and work in a hospital.
SARAH: I’m busy in the morning, but in the evening I’m with my friends. They’re doctors!

MARCO: Hello! I’m Marco.
MARCO: I am a chef, and work in a restaurant in the afternoon.
MARCO: But in the morning, I always talk with my friends and play soccer. It is very fun!

==================================================
TRACK 1.9 — LISTEN AND MATCH THE OCCUPATIONS
==================================================

1.
INTERVIEWER: Hi, Allison. Nice to meet you. So, what do you do?
ALLISON: Hi! Nice to meet you too. I’m a doctor. I work at a hospital near my house.
INTERVIEWER: Oh, nice. Is your family in medicine too?
ALLISON: No! My brother is a teacher. Very different.
INTERVIEWER: A doctor and a teacher. Busy family!
ALLISON: Yes, very busy!

2.
INTERVIEWER: Hello, Mike. What do you do?
MIKE: I’m a farmer. I work outside most days.
INTERVIEWER: Do you like it?
MIKE: Yes, I do. My wife works in town. She’s a receptionist.
INTERVIEWER: So you’re the farmer in the family.
MIKE: That’s right.

3.
INTERVIEWER: Hi, John. Are you a taxi driver?
JOHN: No, that’s my friend Carlos. I’m an office worker.
INTERVIEWER: Ah, okay. Where do you work?
JOHN: In an office downtown. I’m there from Monday to Friday.
INTERVIEWER: Got it. Office worker.
JOHN: Exactly.

4.
INTERVIEWER: Hi, Lisa. You work in a restaurant, right?
LISA: Yes. My brother is a chef there, but I’m a waitress.
INTERVIEWER: Do you work together a lot?
LISA: Almost every day. He cooks, and I take the food to the tables.
INTERVIEWER: Sounds busy.
LISA: It is, especially at night.

5.
INTERVIEWER: Hello, Bryan. What’s your job?
BRYAN: I’m a salesclerk. I work in a small shop.
INTERVIEWER: What do you sell?
BRYAN: Clothes, mostly. My dad is a farmer, so our jobs are completely different.
INTERVIEWER: Very different!
BRYAN: Yes, but I like my job.
