Track six point two.

Two places. More than shops.

New York. New York is a big city in the USA. People travel by train and bus. Grand Central is a famous train station. The New York Transit Museum is in an old station! People look at old trains there. They also walk and run in Central Park.

Stowe. Stowe is a small town in Vermont, USA. People walk and ride bikes on the Recreation Path. They go to the library for books. The town also has museums. The Vermont Ski and Snowboard Museum is about winter sports. Small-town life is not only work and shopping!
