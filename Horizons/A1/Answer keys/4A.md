## Teaching sequence and language limits

Match twelve clear cutouts to everyday food/drink nouns, then encounter the same language in two short breakfast descriptions. Compare singular an egg, plural eggs, and uncountable bread in activity 4 before reading the grammar box. The pilot grammar box illustrates an apple/two apples with exact-count cutouts, and rice/milk in their food/drink senses. Only the singular article and plural ending receive bold crimson emphasis; short rules clarify a/an with one, plural forms with two or more, and no a/an or plural -s in the mass-food examples. One visibly crossed-out a rice is corrected to rice. Its visual contrast supports the sort and picture-correction practice. Rice and eggs are worked examples in the sorting table. Explain that a/an goes with a singular countable noun; plural countable nouns and the uncountable food meanings here take neither a nor an. Recycle the earlier sound-based a/an distinction: an egg/an apple, a banana. Do not add a new adjective-order explanation.

For early A1, fish, cheese, bread, rice, milk, tea, coffee and juice are introduced in their uncountable food/drink senses. Do not teach that these words are uncountable in every possible context. A coffee meaning a serving, cheeses meaning varieties and fish as animals are outside this lesson's scope. Countable eggs, apples, bananas and tomatoes supply plural contrast. Eat is introduced by the title and repeated input; drink and present-simple questions/third-person forms recycle Unit 3. Traditional breakfasts provide cultural context without claiming that all people in a country eat the same meal.

The continuation-page picnic supplies exact visual evidence to correct two deliberate text errors. The final pair task uses unknown personal breakfast habits to agree a small menu, recorded in open fields. It remains an A language lesson, rather than a D writing-skills lesson. No like/dislike/object pronouns (4B), too much/many/enough or recipes (4C), or because/so (4D) is required. No food, health, portion or diet recommendation is made.
