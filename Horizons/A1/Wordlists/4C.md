# 4C — Cooking verbs and quantities

Core verbs: wash, cut, add, mix. Introduce as short recipe instructions: wash the tomatoes; cut the tomatoes; add the cheese and oil; mix the salad.

Core quantity language: too much + uncountable noun; too many + plural countable noun; enough / not enough + either noun type. Enough means sufficient, including more than the minimum, rather than exactly the recipe amount.

Essential contextual support: recipe, salad, spoon/spoons, oil, need, use, more. “For two people” and “spoon of” are functional recipe chunks. Recycle tomato/tomatoes, cheese, eggs, milk, apples, food preferences and personal questions from 4A/4B and earlier lessons.

Instructions stay with listen, repeat, complete, read, look, circle, write, work in pairs, plan, find. No additional kitchen-equipment/heat-treatment vocabulary, adjective + enough, because/so or health claims.
