## Track 8.10 - Activity 2

Jessica: Track eight point ten.

Jessica: [slowly] I'd like a ticket to Toronto, please. [pause] One way or return? [pause] What time does it leave? [pause] How much is it? [pause] Can you repeat that? [pause] Can you spell your name?

## Track 8.11 - Activity 3

Jessica: Track eight point eleven.

MarkNatural: Hello. City Tickets. How can I help?

Adeline: I'd like a ticket to Toronto, please.

MarkNatural: For today?

Adeline: Yes, please. One way.

MarkNatural: There's a flight at three fifteen.

Adeline: Sorry. Can you repeat that?

MarkNatural: Three fifteen. You arrive at four twenty.

Adeline: How much is it?

MarkNatural: One hundred and twenty dollars.

Adeline: That's fine. My name is Nora Lee.

MarkNatural: How do you spell Lee?

Adeline: L, E, E.

MarkNatural: Thank you, Nora. Your flight leaves at three fifteen.
