Advice and a change of plan

8B Give useful advice

PositiveNegativeYes/no question
You should take a coat.You should not take a coat.Should you take a coat?

Negative should → should not / shouldn’t

Yes/no You should → Should you…?

I / you / he / she / it / we / they → should wear · should wears / should to wear

1 Match each situation with advice. Write a, b or c.

  1. It’s raining.
  2. It’s very hot.
  3. It’s cold and windy.

a You should take some water.

b You should wear a warm coat.

c You should take an umbrella.

2 Fix the advice questions and sentences.

  1. Do I should take a coat?
  2. You shouldn’t to go outside.

Have or has?

One part / more than one

My arm hurts.

My arms hurt.

have: I / you / we / they. has: he / she / it.

  1. He have / has a cold.
  2. My head hurt / hurts.
  3. I am / have a headache.
  4. My arms hurt / hurts.

4 Put the words in order. Then write a short message using an invented problem.

  1. a / have / I / cold
  2. My / hurts / leg
