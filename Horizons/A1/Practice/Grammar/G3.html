  1. I get up at six. → She .

Do you? Does she?3B

I / you / we / they

I don’t work on Sunday.

Do you work on Sunday?

Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

He / she / it

She doesn’t work on Sunday.

Does she work on Sunday?

Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.

After does or doesn’t, use work, not works: Does she works? → Does she work?

  2. Leo eats breakfast at home. → Leo .

Ask for the missing detail3C

Where → a place Where do you go? To the park.

Who → a person Who do you go with? My friend.

When → a day or time When do you go? On Friday.

What time → a clock time What time do you go? At six.

  1. do you work? At Green Café.
  2. does Ana have lunch? At twelve.
  3. do you go with? My sister.
