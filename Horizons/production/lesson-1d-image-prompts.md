# Horizons A1 — Lesson 1D Image Prompts

Lesson: **1D — LET’S FILL A FORM!**  
Focus: personal information + capitalization  
Prototype: `../examples/lesson-1d-canonical-prototype.html`

These prompts are production art direction for the raster photography placeholders currently used in Lesson 1D. They follow the theme-neutral rules in `../design-system/CANONICAL-STYLE.md` and `../design-system/asset-policy.md`.

## Shared visual direction

Use **photorealistic editorial photography** with believable environments, natural human expressions, realistic materials and contemporary everyday styling. Each image should feel like a credible photograph from the world it represents, not like a staged language-learning scene.

Do **not** add a school, classroom or educational theme unless the scene itself calls for one. Avoid obvious stock-photo gestures, exaggerated smiles, floating graphics, fake interface overlays, visible lesson text, watermarks, logos, brand marks, cartoon styling or vector illustration.

Keep important faces, hands and contextual objects away from crop edges. Leave useful negative space where specified. Avoid legible signage unless specifically requested; the page supplies its own typography and forms.

## HZN_A1_U01_LD_E01_IMG_01 — Seaside Hotel check-in

**Use:** Exercise 1 hero image  
**Current slot:** tall environmental crop, approximately portrait/vertical, clipped slightly on the right edge  
**Purpose:** make the hotel check-in situation immediately believable before the learner reads the conversation and form.

### Prompt

Photorealistic editorial hospitality photograph inside a contemporary coastal boutique hotel reception area. A friendly adult hotel receptionist stands behind a real reception counter speaking naturally with an adult female guest who has just arrived, both shown in a candid three-quarter interaction rather than posing for the camera. The guest has a small travel bag or carry-on beside her; the receptionist is attentive and professional. Subtle coastal-hotel atmosphere through light wood, stone, soft daylight, a glimpse of plants or seaside-inspired interior details, but no obvious nautical clichés. Frame the people and counter in a vertical environmental composition, with the main human interaction concentrated from center-left to center and enough clean background context to instantly read as a hotel lobby. Natural daylight mixed with warm interior lighting, realistic skin texture, documentary-lifestyle photography, sophisticated but accessible mid-range hotel, contemporary clothing, authentic proportions, 35mm-lens feel, moderate depth of field, crisp enough for A4 print.

**Composition constraints:** portrait-oriented master; keep both faces and upper bodies comfortably inside the central 70% of the frame; preserve environmental context above and below; avoid placing critical details at the far right because the production crop uses an angled right edge.

**Exclude:** classroom, school props, books, worksheets, exaggerated customer-service gestures, handshake pose, looking at camera, visible hotel brand logo, readable signs, giant key props, luxury-palace aesthetic, cartoon look, CGI sheen, watermark, text.

**Purpose:** instantly communicate `Gym` without relying on a label.

### Prompt

Photorealistic lifestyle photograph of a modern neighborhood gym reception area, designed to remain recognizable inside a small circular crop. Show a compact front desk in the foreground with unmistakable gym context immediately behind it: strength or cardio equipment, a training area, and one or two casually active adults in the background. Contemporary, welcoming, energetic but not luxury-fitness-club advertising. **Use:** Exercise 5 business choice

### Prompt

Photorealistic editorial photograph of the reception or welcome area of a small contemporary language institute. ## HZN_A1_U01_LD_E07_IMG_01 — Real role-play at a reception desk

**Use:** Exercise 7 role-play support image  
**Current slot:** wide horizontal crop with a slightly angled right edge  
**Purpose:** model the physical interaction learners are about to perform: one person acts as staff and another as customer/guest/member while information is exchanged and recorded.

### Prompt

Photorealistic candid editorial photograph of two late-teen or young-adult classmates performing a realistic reception-desk role-play in a flexible training or community space. One person is seated or standing behind a simple temporary reception counter acting as staff and is writing information on a paper form or clipboard while speaking to the other person, who stands on the customer side answering naturally. Their body language should clearly show an information-exchange transaction: eye contact, attentive listening, pen and form in use, relaxed professional interaction. The physical setup may be simple and improvised, but should visually resemble a small real service desk rather than a conventional classroom exercise. Keep any classroom-specific cues very subtle and secondary; the image should primarily read as `staff member checking in a customer`. Contemporary casual clothing, diverse believable young adults, natural expressions, soft daylight, documentary photography, realistic skin and hands, 35mm-lens feel.

**Composition constraints:** landscape master image; place both people and the writing action in the left and center portions of the frame; preserve some clean environmental space on the right; keep faces, hands, pen and form fully visible; ensure the interaction remains readable after a relatively shallow horizontal crop.

**Exclude:** people holding textbooks, teacher supervising, students raising hands, blackboard as dominant background, obvious staged English-class imagery, both people looking at camera, handshake pose, readable personal data on the form, visible logos, text overlay, watermark, illustration, CGI look.

## Production notes

- Generate or source all six images as raster assets.
- Recommended master size: at least 2000 px on the shortest side before crop/export.
- Keep original masters before production crops.
- Final asset filenames should use the IDs above with `.webp` unless production requires another raster format.
- Exercise 5 images are a repeated family: use comparable exposure, realism and photographic quality, but **do not force the four businesses into the same literal location or theme**.
- The role-play image is the only image in this set where a learning/training environment may appear, because the actual task involves classmates rehearsing a real transaction. Even there, the visual emphasis should remain on the transaction rather than on `education` as a theme.
