HORIZONS A1

UNIT 1: New Friends
A: Hi, I’m Bri! (The verb to be, alphabet, numbers)
B: What’s your job? (Occupations, verb to be contractions)
C: Where are you from? (Countries, adjectives)
D: Let’s fill a form! (Personal info, capitalization)

UNIT 2: What’s in My Bag?
A: Look inside my bag! (Personal items, demonstrative pronouns)
B: What time is the next bus? (Telling time, articles) - Real transport apps such as Moovit and InDrive and how people use them.
C: Meet my family! (Family members, possessive case) - Based on real interviews about family businesses linking to jobs and occupations.
D: Is that my phone? (Colors, adjectives) - Asking about possessions in a Lost and Found office.

UNIT 3: My Life
A: What’s your routine? (Present simple "+", daily routine) - A day in the life of someone famous.
B: Weekdays or weekends? (Days of the week, present simple "-" "?“) - How different countries structure their workweeks, like siesta in Spain and overtime in Japan.
C: Where do you go? (Wh- questions, adjectives) - Google Maps reviews.
D: My week in order (Frequency adverbs, using connectors ‘after’, ‘before’) - A social media challenge about healthy daily habits.

UNIT 4: Let’s Eat!
A: What do you eat? (Food and drink, uncountable and countable) - Traditional meals around the world.
B: Tasty or not? (Flavors, like/don’t like, object pronouns) - The rise of vegan food trends.
C: Cooking time! (Common verbs, too much/many and enough) - Beginner-friendly cooking guides.
D: Food for everyone! (Punctuation, using connectors ‘because’, ‘so’) - Stories of food sharing apps and waste reduction.

UNIT 5: Shopping Time
A: What are you wearing? (Clothes and accessories, present continuous) - A real story about a lucky thrift shop find.
B: That’s my favorite! (Shopping verbs, adjectives, too and enough) - Reviews of online shopping experiences.
C: How often do you shop? (Frequency expressions, places in town) - Minimalist living and spending habits.
D: At the store (Going grocery shopping) - Cashier-less stores and how they work compared to traditional ones.

UNIT 6: Moving Around
A: City life or country life? (Places in town 2) - Comparing two real small and big city lifestyles.
B: My dream home (Prepositions of place, rooms, and furniture) - Tiny homes and people who live in them.
C: My neighborhood (Prepositions of movement, giving directions) - Walkability scores and how people rate their areas.
D: Where's the mall? (Can/can’t, describing a city) - Exploring the world’s most unique shopping centers.

UNIT 7: Back in the Day
A: When I was little… (Past of verb to be) - Nostalgic interviews about childhood in different decades.
B: I was born in July! (Dates, ordinal numbers, ago) - Celebrity birthdays and their stories.
C: Life in the 80s (Regular past verbs "+", "-", "?“) - People sharing their past life experiences online.
D: Dear Diary (Irregular past verbs, writing about your day, sequencers) - Famous personal diaries through history.

UNIT 8: Travel Adventures
A: Let’s go to Toronto! (Travel verbs, animals) - Real travel vlogs about city experiences.
B: It’s so cold! (Weather and seasons, should/shouldn’t) - News articles about extreme weather events.
C: Can you go tomorrow? (Health complaints, body parts) - Real cases of people postponing trips due to health.
D: Tickets, please! (Phone language, buying a ticket) - Stories of people booking last-minute flights.
