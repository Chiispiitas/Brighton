# 5D - Grocery shopping

Explicit new words, pictured and recorded: cashier, customer, checkout, cash, card, receipt. The contrast between a person (cashier/customer), a place/equipment (checkout), payment objects (cash/card) and the resulting document (receipt) is made visually explicit. Receipt is modeled with its normal silent p; no spelling test or extra pronunciation rule is imposed.

New functional shopping chunks: Do you have any eggs?; I'd like some bread and some milk, please; Anything else?; No, thanks; How much is it?; That's five dollars; Can I pay by card?; Here's your receipt; Excuse me. How do I pay?; Can I get a receipt? These are the new speaking content, made visible in the model, matching and SKILLS box. Any/some and Can I...? are supported whole frames, not a new quantifier chapter or ability-can lesson brought forward from Unit 6.

Recycled food: bread, milk, eggs, apples, rice, cheese. Recycled concepts: buy, pay, have, use, choose, work, food, store, prices, numbers, questions with do, polite please/thank you and because for an optional reason. Whole-dollar prices are simplified fictional classroom data; roleplay must communicate actual unknown choices, stock and prices. Teacher supports substitutions after Sorry, we don't.

Cashier-free is a supported compound in the short contextual comparison: payment is automatic and employees still help. It does not mean free food or staff-free. Automatically is a transparent contextual cognate; technology, leave and online receipts are supported receptive context rather than a new tested technology vocabulary set. The comparison is present-tense description and a current help interaction, not past narration. No regional store list, biometric/payment-account setup, banking field, aisle-direction system or unsupported sensor terminology is introduced.

Instructions use established read/listen/match/circle/underline/choose/write/ask/work-in-pairs/change-roles frames. Private means do not show the actual choices to the partner. Separate paper makes the information gap real even though both role instructions are visible. The communication reference is a future back-of-book destination, not a claim that the backmatter is already paginated.
