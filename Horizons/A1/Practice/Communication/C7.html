A day worth writing about

Turn a small event into a readable story 7D

JUNE6Saturday

The empty café

Saturday, June 6th

First, I went to a café with my friend. It was closed. Then we walked to the park. We saw a small music show there. After that, we bought some bread and ate in the park. Finally, I went home. A closed café was the start of a good day!

1Read the diary. Answer in a few words.

  1. What changed the plan?
  2. Why was it still a good day?

2Repair this diary opening. Use capitals, punctuation and the past of go.

yesterday i go to the park then i saw my friend

3Write about one day: real or imagined. Give it a title.

Choose a small surprise, a change of plan or one thing you enjoyed. Tell the events in order. Use past verbs and two or more of these words.

firstthenafter thatfinally

Reader check: Give the diary to a partner. Ask them to suggest a different title and ask one question about the day. Revise one sentence if it is unclear.
