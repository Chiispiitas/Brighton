# 3C - Where do you go?

Unit 3: My Life. Focus: present-simple questions with where, when, who ... with and what time; adjectives for places. Builds on 3A affirmative routines, 3B do/does, days of the week, 2B times, and 2D adjective position. Does not introduce frequency adverbs, before/after, why/because or a general places-in-town syllabus.

Extra Practice: personal answers. Ask a family member the two printed questions and write the answers.

## Teaching sequence

The review feature introduces target adjectives in a recognizable setting. Locate and pronounce them in activity 2, then use the Adjectives vocabulary reference for further practice. Recycle adjective location from 2D when needed. Use the 3.7 conversation as direct evidence for question words and do/does. Read it once for Ravi's place, then connect each question to its answer in activity 4. The grammar focus on page 28 clarifies the pattern; activities 5 and 6 move from meaning to word order. Activity 7 recycles adjective location and a/an. The information gap gives learners a reason to ask third-person questions. The final review transfers the language to a place they actually go to.

Only the four supported question frames are production targets. Who does ... go with? asks about a companion; do not broaden this into subject questions such as Who goes...? Avoid formula-like discovery prompts. Use the visible sentences, bold forms and answers.
