Lesson3C

Where do you go?

Ask about a place and recommend it.

Ask for the missing detail3C

Where → a place Where do you go? To the park.

Who → a person Who do you go with? My friend.

When → a day or time When do you go? On Friday.

What time → a clock time What time do you go? At six.

1 Complete with where, what time or who.

  1. do you work? At Green Café.
  2. does Ana have lunch? At twelve.
  3. do you go with? My sister.

2 Put the words in order.

on Saturday / where / go / do / you / ?
