## How lessons and banks work

The main lesson introduces a coherent, manageable selection in context. Its Vocabulary link opens the fuller useful A1 topic bank: familiar words plus additional relevant words, clear pictures or meanings, then original retrieval activities. Four to six items is a useful starting point, not a mechanical ceiling. Larger contextual sets need a documented reason; closed sets such as the alphabet, days and months stay coherent. Do not pad these sets or move the full bank into the main lesson.

Grammar references organize the complete relevant taught system with labelled charts, aligned forms, short answers and selective real diagrams. They may repeat lesson examples. They must not add unrelated advanced grammar just to look larger. Compressed arrow/dot/slash prose remains prohibited as a substitute for a chart.
