MARA’S SHOPPING NOTES

Before I buy

How often do you go to the supermarket?
Once a week. I go every Saturday.
And the market?
Twice a month. I buy apples and tomatoes there.
How often do you buy clothes?
Once a year. Before I buy clothes, I ask, “Do I need them?”
And books?
I don’t buy books. I go to the library three times a month.
