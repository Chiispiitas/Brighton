Part 4 - Picture multiple choice

[clear] Look at the pictures. Listen and choose the correct picture. [pause]

[clear] Sixteen. What is Laura wearing to the concert? [short pause]

[happy] Are you ready for the concert, Laura?

[thoughtful] Almost. I wanted to wear my new dress, but it’s cold tonight.

[curious] So what are you wearing?

[happy] My black jacket and my blue skirt.

[curious] Nice! [long pause]

[clear] Seventeen. How is Marco going to the train station? [short pause]

[curious] Marco, are you taking the bus to the train station?

[thoughtful] I usually do, but I’m late today.

[curious] Are you walking?

[chuckles] No way! I’m getting a taxi.

[happy] Good idea. [long pause]

[clear] Eighteen. What was the weather like at the beach yesterday? [short pause]

[curious] Did you have a good time at the beach yesterday?

[happy] Yes, but the weather was strange.

[curious] Was it raining?

[thoughtful] No. It was sunny in the morning, but at the beach it was very windy.

[curious] Very windy?

[happy] Yes. My hat went across the sand! [long pause]

[clear] Nineteen. What did Mia buy at the market? [short pause]

[curious] Did you buy those boots at the market, Mia?

[happy] No. They were too expensive.

[curious] What about the sweater?

[thoughtful] I liked it, but I didn’t buy it. I bought this hat.

[happy] Oh, the red hat. It looks great! [long pause]

[clear] Twenty. What are they doing this evening? [short pause]

[curious] Do you want to play videogames tonight?

[thoughtful] Maybe tomorrow. There’s a comedy I want to see.

[curious] At the movie theater?

[happy] Yes. Let’s watch the movie this evening.

[curious] Great! [long pause]
