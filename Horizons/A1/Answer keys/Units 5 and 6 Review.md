## 6 Your message

Open answers. Assess a coherent original message to another friend planning a surprise: place, time, clothes and a purchase, using familiar present, can, directions and shopping language where useful. Do not require all grammar items in one message. Eight open 7mm lines preserve expression; no sentence-gap final-writing template. Fictional details are acceptable; no home address, off-site visit, real purchase, account, message sending or actual surprise party is required.

Possible model (not printed): Hi, Ana! We’re at my home on Sunday at five. Wear your red dress. Can you buy some apples? You can pay by card at the market. My home is next to the park. See you! Jo.
