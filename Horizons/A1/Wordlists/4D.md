# 4D - Food for everyone!

New lexical chunks: **share food**, **free** (no money, $0), **waste food** (good food is not eaten or shared). Printed short glosses anchor the three meanings; teach waste in the context of usable food, not unavoidable inedible parts.

Writing targets: **because** introduces the reason; **so** introduces the result. Model reason after the main clause; use a comma before so when joining these complete clauses. Do not introduce initial because clauses or imply that because never permits a comma in other constructions.

Punctuation: capital letter at the beginning of a sentence, capital I and names; full stop; question mark; comma before so in these models. Retain the apostrophes already met in I'm and don't, without adding an apostrophe lesson.

Recycle: apples, bread, bananas, milk, food, too much / too many, need, want, eat, have, place, time, near, cafe, station, at and from ... to ... . No new food taxonomy, future tense, passive voice or app-interface vocabulary syllabus. **Post** is the necessary writing artifact; food-sharing app is the visible context. Reason/result metalanguage stays out of learner instructions.
