HORIZONS A1 — LESSON 2B
WHAT TIME IS THE NEXT BUS?

==================================================
TRACK 2.3 — HORIZONS ON AIR
EPISODE: BUS OR CAR?
==================================================

[HORIZONS ON AIR STING]

HOST: Welcome back to Horizons On Air! Today we’re in Manta, Ecuador, talking about how people move around the city. Bus, car, app... everyone has a different routine. Let’s find out what people use.

HOST: Hi there! What’s your name?
JULIA: I’m Julia. Nice to meet you.
HOST: Nice to meet you too, Julia. Where are you going this morning?
JULIA: I’m going to work. I usually take the bus because it’s easy and cheap.
HOST: And how do you check the bus time?
JULIA: I use Moovit. It’s a public transport app. It shows me the bus number and the time.
HOST: That sounds useful. What time is your bus today?
JULIA: Six twenty.
HOST: Pretty early! And what number is it?
JULIA: Bus fifteen.
HOST: Bus fifteen at six twenty. Do you take that bus every day?
JULIA: Almost every day. Sometimes I get a taxi, but not today.
HOST: Great. Thanks, Julia. Have a good day at work!
JULIA: Thanks! You too.

[STREET AMBIENCE]

HOST: Hi! Can I ask you a quick question?
DIEGO: Sure.
HOST: What’s your name?
DIEGO: Diego.
HOST: Diego, are you waiting for a bus too?
DIEGO: No, not today. I’m running a little late, so I’m using inDrive.
HOST: Okay, so what is inDrive exactly?
DIEGO: It’s a ride app. You can ask for a car, choose a price, and then a driver comes to pick you up.
HOST: Nice. Is your car on the way now?
DIEGO: Yes. It should be here at six forty.
HOST: And how do you know which car is yours?
DIEGO: I can see the plate in the app. Mine is MAB-3997.
HOST: MAB-3997. Got it. Do you normally use inDrive?
DIEGO: Not every day. I usually take the bus, but when I’m late, a car is faster.
HOST: Fair enough. Hope you make it on time!
DIEGO: Me too! Thanks.

[STREET AMBIENCE]

HOST: And here’s one more person. Hi! What’s your name?
ANA: Hi, I’m Ana.
HOST: Ana, bus or car today?
ANA: Bus. I’m not in a hurry, so I’m taking the bus.
HOST: Do you use an app for that?
ANA: Yes, I use Moovit too. I check the time before I leave home.
HOST: So Julia uses Moovit, and you use Moovit as well.
ANA: Yes. It’s very useful.
HOST: What time is your bus?
ANA: Seven thirty.
HOST: And the number?
ANA: Number eight.
HOST: Bus eight at seven thirty. Is that your usual bus?
ANA: Yes, usually. Sometimes my sister drives me, but today she has work.
HOST: So it’s the bus today.
ANA: Exactly.
HOST: Thanks, Ana. Have a nice morning!
ANA: You too. Bye!

[HORIZONS ON AIR STING]

HOST: So, what did we hear today? Julia and Ana both use Moovit and take the bus. Diego is the different one today: he’s using inDrive and going by car. Three people, three schedules, one busy morning in Manta. This is Horizons On Air. See you next time!
